## rex CameraController (phaser3-rex-notes) — camera pan / pinch-zoom method

The touch controller in `v2.js` (pan: `scroll -= fingerDelta / zoom`; pinch: `zoom *= distance / previousDistance` with the
focus world point kept under the fingers' midpoint) follows the method of rex's PanScroll and PinchZoom components.
Source: https://github.com/rexrainbow/phaser3-rex-notes (plugins/camera/panscroll, plugins/camera/pinchzoom). No assets were used.
